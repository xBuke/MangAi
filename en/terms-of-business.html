1. Quote and contract

  • Every job starts with a written quote stating scope, price, deadline and payment terms. A quote is valid for 30 days unless it says otherwise.
  • Work starts on written acceptance of the quote (email is enough), a purchase order or a signed contract.
  • Changes in scope during the work are agreed in writing, together with their effect on price and deadline.

2. Prices and payment

  • Prices are in euros. The business is not registered for VAT, so no VAT is charged under Art. 90(1) of the Croatian VAT Act.
  • Invoices are issued as e-invoices and are payable within the period stated in the quote or invoice.
  • Larger projects may be paid in phases or with an advance, as stated in the quote. Maintenance is billed monthly or as agreed.

3. Collaboration and deadlines

  • Work is done in phases with regular progress reviews. The number of revision rounds per phase is stated in the quote.
  • The client provides content, access and feedback on time. If these are late, the deadline moves by the same amount.

4. Handover and warranty

  • Handover is confirmed by a handover record or written confirmation. If the client reports no defects within 8 days of delivery, the delivery is deemed accepted.
  • Defects in the delivered work are fixed free of charge for 3 months after handover, unless the quote says otherwise.
  • The warranty does not cover new features, changes made by others, or changes in external services (such as hosting or third-party APIs).

5. Source code and copyright

The aim is that you are not tied to me after the work is done. Rights are split as follows:

What you receive

  • The source code of everything built specifically for you, documentation, access credentials (hosting, domain, admin) and all project materials.
  • An exclusive right of use, unlimited in time and territory, to those parts, including the right to modify, adapt and copy them and to have other contractors maintain and extend them.
  • Domain, hosting and user accounts are registered in your name wherever possible.

What MangAI keeps

  • General knowledge, experience and working methods.
  • Its own tools, templates and components created before the project or independently of it and reused across projects. For these you receive a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free right to use and modify them within the delivered system, also after the collaboration ends.
  • The right to list the project as a reference (client name, short description and screenshots) unless you request confidentiality.

Open source and external services

  • Open-source components (such as software libraries or WordPress) are used under their own licences, which allow you to use and modify them.
  • External services (hosting, AI models, email services) are subject to their providers' terms. Subscriptions are paid by the client unless agreed otherwise.

Rights pass to the client on payment of the full agreed amount. Until then the client may use the delivery for review and handover. The author's moral rights under Croatian copyright law remain with the author, but are not used to restrict your use of the delivery.

6. Use of AI tools

I use AI tools to develop and write faster. All code and text is reviewed before handover. Your confidential and personal data is not entered into AI tools that use it to train models.

7. Confidentiality and data protection

Information obtained during the work is kept confidential, also after the collaboration ends. When I process personal data on your behalf, we sign a data processing agreement under Art. 28 of the GDPR. See the privacy policy.

8. Liability

Liability for damage caused by my work is limited to the amount paid for that service in the last 12 months, except for damage caused intentionally or by gross negligence. I am not liable for lost profit, outages of external services beyond my control, or content published by the client.

9. Termination

  • A project can be terminated by written notice. Work done until termination is paid, and the client receives everything built so far.
  • A maintenance contract can be cancelled with 30 days' notice. On termination, all access and the latest backup are handed over.

10. Governing law and disputes

Croatian law applies. Disputes are settled by agreement where possible; otherwise the competent court in the Republic of Croatia has jurisdiction under the general rules, unless the contract provides otherwise.

Changes

These terms may change. Work already agreed is governed by the terms in force when the quote was accepted. Questions can be sent to marko@mangai.hr.
