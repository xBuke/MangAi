How to show up in ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews: a guide for small businesses in Croatia

Updated 10 November 2026 – 8 min read

Contents

People increasingly type a whole question instead of two keywords: “which company does AI automation in Croatia?” or “what affordable consultants can improve my visibility in AI chat results?”. The answer comes from Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity or Copilot, with links to a few sources. Optimising for these answers is called AEO (answer engine optimisation) or GEO (generative engine optimisation). The good news for small businesses: it mostly comes down to solid SEO and clearly written content.

How AI search tools choose sources

When a question needs current information, AI assistants search the web and build the answer from the pages they find. So it helps to know who reads what:

  • Google AI Overviews and AI Mode use Google’s index. Google says there are no additional requirements or special optimisations: a page must be indexed and eligible to be shown with a snippet.
  • ChatGPT search uses OpenAI’s OAI-SearchBot. According to OpenAI’s documentation, sites that opt out of it are not shown in ChatGPT search answers. GPTBot is used for model training and is controlled separately.
  • Perplexity uses its own crawler, PerplexityBot, to find and link pages in its answers.
  • Microsoft Copilot relies on Bing.

Eight steps that actually help

1. Let the crawlers in

Check robots.txt and do not block Googlebot, Bingbot, OAI-SearchBot or PerplexityBot. If you use Cloudflare or a similar service, also check its AI bot blocking settings. You can block GPTBot to keep your content out of training and still appear in ChatGPT search.

2. Submit your site to Google and Bing

Add the site to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools and submit your sitemap. Bing also supports IndexNow, which notifies it immediately about new or changed pages.

3. Keep your Google Business Profile up to date

Google explicitly recommends keeping Business Profile information current. Category, services, service area, hours and description should match your website.

4. Answer the question straight away

If someone asks “how long does a website take?”, the answer belongs in the first sentence under the heading, with the explanation after it. Question-style headings and a short FAQ help both people and AI find the answer. Look at the queries in your Search Console performance report: more and more of them are full sentences.

5. Be specific

AI tools prefer pages that clearly say who you are, what you do, for whom, where and on what terms. “We provide innovative digital solutions” answers nothing. “We build accessible websites for municipalities, delivered in 4 to 8 weeks” answers three questions.

6. Add honest structured data

Google says no special schema.org markup is needed for AI features. Structured data about your business, articles and FAQs still helps search engines understand the page, as long as it matches the visible content.

7. Get mentioned beyond your own site

When AI compiles a list of companies, it draws on several sources: business directories, industry associations, local media, partner sites and reviews. Keep your name, location, contact and description consistent everywhere.

8. Measure

Traffic from Google’s AI features is included in the Search Console performance report. Visits from ChatGPT usually show up in analytics with chatgpt.com as the source. Once a month, ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google the questions your customers ask and note whether you are mentioned and which pages are cited.

What does not help

  • Special “AI files”. Google says no new machine-readable files are needed. Files such as llms.txt do no harm, but there is no confirmation that major search tools use them to pick sources.
  • Mass-produced articles. Dozens of similar texts with no new value can be treated as spam and hurt the whole site.
  • Hidden text and keyword stuffing. These did not work in classic SEO either.

Limiting what AI shows

Google supports nosnippet, data-nosnippet and max-snippet to limit what appears in results and AI features, while noindex removes a page from search entirely. For OpenAI, access is set in robots.txt, separately for search and for training.

Common questions

What is AEO?

AEO (answer engine optimisation) means making your content easy for AI assistants and AI search features, such as ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews, to find, understand and cite. It mostly comes down to good SEO and clear answers to customer questions.

Do I need special optimisation for Google AI Overviews?

According to Google, no. A page must be indexed and eligible for a snippet, and the usual SEO best practices apply.

How do I appear in ChatGPT search?

Do not block OAI-SearchBot in robots.txt, make sure the site is indexed and answers questions clearly. Blocking GPTBot for training does not affect search visibility.
