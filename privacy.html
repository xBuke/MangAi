I don't send marketing messages without your explicit consent and I don't sell data to anyone.

Cookies and analytics

The site uses no tracking, advertising or analytics cookies. The demos remember your chosen language and tab in your browser's local storage; that data stays on your device and is not sent to me.

Who else processes data

I use external providers to run the site and communications. They process data only on my behalf:

  • Vercel Inc. – website hosting (servers may be outside the EU; transfers rely on Vercel's data processing agreement with EU standard contractual clauses).
  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) – delivering messages from the contact form and advisor to my email.
  • The email provider for addresses on the mangai.hr domain.
  • Google Fonts – on blog pages only, which load fonts from Google's servers, so Google sees your IP address.

How long I keep data

  • Enquiries that did not lead to work: up to two years after the last contact, then deleted.
  • Contracts, invoices and related records: as long as tax and accounting rules require.
  • Server logs: as long as the hosting provider keeps them, usually briefly.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, correction, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interest. Send requests to marko@mangai.hr; I reply within one month at the latest.

If you believe your data was processed unlawfully, you can complain to the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (azop.hr).

Data I process for clients

When I build or maintain a system for a client that processes their users' personal data (for example citizen forms or an AI assistant), the client is the controller and I am the processor. We set this out in a separate data processing agreement.

Changes

This policy may change when the site or the regulations change. The date at the top shows the latest update.
