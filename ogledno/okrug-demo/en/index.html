Heritage trail through the centre of Okrug Gornji

Trail length and duration: 6 stops · 1.5 km · about 23 min on foot

Loading the map…

Saves the map of the trail area to this device.

An easy walk from the church of St Theodore on the shore, along the beach and the little harbour, to the churches and the municipal building in the centre. Six stops, a map in your pocket and stories that fit into a few sentences.

Stops on the trail

  1. 11. Church of St Theodore (Sv. Tudor)A small church on the shore, at the head of Saldun Bay, dedicated to the patron saint of Okrug.
  2. 22. Copacabana BeachA pebble beach along the promenade, part of Okrug's long shoreline.
  3. 33. The little harbour and Toć coveA small harbour at the end of the beach and Toć cove, the stage for summer festivities.
  4. 44. Church of St Charles BorromeoA church from 1735 with a bell gable, north of the old road to Trogir.
  5. 55. Okrug municipal buildingThe seat of the Municipality of Okrug, at Bana Jelačića 17.
  6. 66. Parish Church of the Exaltation of the Holy CrossThe parish church of the parish of St Charles Borromeo.
