Bočići cove

A small cove on the south-eastern edge, the first with a view of the open sea.

Here the path turns south, and Bočići is a small cove with a beach on the south-eastern side of Okrug.

From this side of Čiovo the view opens towards the Split Channel and the islands offshore.
