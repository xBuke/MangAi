Racettini family summer residence (Racetinovac)

The only preserved noble summer residence on Čiovo, in Pantera cove.

Pantera cove is home to the summer residence of the Racettini family, an extinct noble family from Trogir. According to the historian Pavao Andreis it was built in 1650.

Next to the one-storey house are the vaulted chapel of St Anthony the Abbot, a cistern, a stable, a courtyard, garden terraces and a threshing floor.

It is the only preserved summer residence of its kind on Čiovo, although noble families had been building them since the end of the 15th century. The marker stands at the head of Racetinovac cove; the exact position of the ruins is still being confirmed.

Did you know?

The cove of Racetinovac is named after the Racettini family.
