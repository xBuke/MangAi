Church of St Charles Borromeo

A church from 1735 with a bell gable, north of the old road to Trogir.

The church of St Charles Borromeo stands north of the old Okrug–Trogir road. The parish also bears the name of St Charles Borromeo.

Manuscripts of the Bishop of Trogir from 1640 mention it as “recently built”. It probably began as a votive chapel after the plague, and the feast of St Charles has been celebrated here since 1610.

The old chapel fell into ruin and was pulled down. The present church was built in 1735 thanks to the villagers and the vicar Nikola Pavković, and the year is carved in stone.

It is a single-nave church with a rectangular apse and a bell gable. The baptismal font with a carved fish probably comes from the old church of St Theodore, and the main altar holds a painting of Our Lady of Mount Carmel.
