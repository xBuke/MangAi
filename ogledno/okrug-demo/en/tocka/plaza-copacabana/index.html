Copacabana Beach

A pebble beach along the promenade, part of Okrug's long shoreline.

Copacabana is a pebble beach along the Stjepan Radić promenade and part of Okrug's long stretch of beach.

Pebble beaches with pines and tamarisks run along the whole length of Okrug.

Did you know?

Officially this is Toć beach: two kilometres of pebbles and promenade. Locals and visitors call it the “Copacabana of Okrug”.
