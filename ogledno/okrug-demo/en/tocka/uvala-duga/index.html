Duga cove and the view of Sveta Fumija

End of the walk: a sheltered anchorage and a view of an islet with an early Christian church.

The walk ends above Duga cove. The Okrug Tourist Board describes it as a sheltered anchorage, and the Šepurina well is nearby.

In front of you lies the islet of Sveta Fumija, some three hundred metres off the coast. It holds the ruins of the early Christian church of St Euphemia, monastery walls, a cistern and a cave where water was collected.

The islet was an estate of the Benedictines of Trogir and an early hermitage, and finds of Roman pottery and amphorae show there was an ancient estate here as well.
