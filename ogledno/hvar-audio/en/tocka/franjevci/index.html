Franciscan monastery and Church of Our Lady of Mercy

A 15th-century Gothic church, a statue by Nikola Firentinac above the portal and the tomb of the poet Lucić.

Text of the story

You have reached the last stop of the tour: the Franciscan monastery and the Church of Our Lady of Mercy. Take a little more time here: there is plenty to see both outside and inside.

The church was built in the 15th century as a single-nave Gothic building. It was raised on the site of an older chapel of the Holy Cross.

Before you go in, raise your eyes above the main portal. There is a statue of the Virgin and Child, the work of Nikola Firentinac. It was put in place between 1465 and 1471.

The church was later extended: in 1536 the northern aisle was built, with the Renaissance chapel of the Holy Cross.

The church also holds the tomb of the poet Hanibal Lucić. You saw his summer residence earlier on the tour, so his story comes to a close right here.

In the monastery refectory there is the painting “The Last Supper” by Matteo Ponzoni. The monastery also has a library and a museum collection.

This is where our tour ends. Thank you for listening. If you especially liked one of the stories, go back and listen to it again. Enjoy the rest of your day in Hvar.
