How a painting on glass is made

The Hlebine painters painted in oil on the back of a sheet of glass. That is why they worked in reverse order: whatever appears on top from the front had to be painted first. Step through the stages and turn the glass over.

A drawing lies under the glass. The painter first draws outlines, leaves, windows and eyes with a fine brush.

Since 2012, painting in oil on glass in the manner of the Hlebine School has been protected as intangible cultural heritage of Croatia (Z-5778).

From glaže to the world

How a village in Podravina became a name in art history.

  1. 1671.

    Hlebine is first mentioned as a village in the Military Frontier.

  2. 1909.

    After his father's death, the Hegedušić family moves back from Petrinja to Hlebine. Krsto is eight.

  3. 1927.

    In Paris, Hegedušić looks at Valentine Prax's paintings on glass and is reminded of the glaže of Podravina.

  4. 1929.

    The Zemlja (Earth) association of artists is founded in Zagreb. Hegedušić is its secretary and driving force.

  5. 1930.

    In Hlebine, Hegedušić notices the drawings of sixteen-year-old Ivan Generalić. Generalić paints his first picture, a village scene in the snow.

  6. 1931.

    At Zemlja's third exhibition, in the Art Pavilion in Zagreb, Generalić and Mraz exhibit for the first time. That year is taken as the beginning of the Hlebine School.

  7. 1936.–1939.

    Mirko Virius from Đelekovec paints his entire body of work. He is killed in the Zemun camp in 1943.

  8. 1953.

    Generalić spends two months in Paris and returns to Hlebine.

  9. 1954.

    Generalić paints a fresco in the atrium of the village school.

  10. 1968.

    On 12 May the Gallery of Naive Art opens in Hlebine.

  11. 2012.

    Painting in oil on glass in the manner of the Hlebine School becomes protected intangible cultural heritage.

  12. 2026.

    The Municipality announces a heritage trail through naive art. 26 November marks 125 years since Krsto Hegedušić was born.

Three generations, one village

The painters were born here and learned from one another here. Their works are protected by copyright, so we do not show them; the links lead to collections where you can see them. The exception is Mirko Virius: he died in 1943, so his works are in the public domain and we show them here.

The Hlebine circle

Today the Hlebine School counts more than two hundred names, and its painters also live in Gola, Molve, Koprivnica, Đelekovec and other villages of Podravina. The map shows places that can be linked to the trail.
