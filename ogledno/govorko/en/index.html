The Govorko family

We have always made wine. Since 2014 it has been in bottles on the market.

Our winery grew out of a love of wine and a wish to keep what our elders left us. The grapes come from vineyards more than fifty years old in the Vrgorac wine region, one of the largest in Croatia.

Next to the old vines of Plavka, Zlatarica Vrgorska, Maraština and Trbljan, we have planted Trnak, Pošip and Merlot.

“Our goal is to return our native grapes to the place they deserve on the Croatian wine map.”
