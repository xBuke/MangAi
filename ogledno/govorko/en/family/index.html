The Govorko family

It all started with a truck outside the house.

One morning a truck pulled up outside my dad’s house in Metković. The driver rang the bell and dad called me in Zagreb to ask what was going on. I told him to carry everything into the old butcher shop downstairs and not to worry.

In the truck were ten used oak barrels my brothers and I had found while I was finishing my agronomy degree. Those barrels pretty much led us to where we are today.

For years, grapes from Dusina were sold to middlemen at the markets. I told dad: let’s keep them and make wine. If it doesn’t sell, we’ll drink it. It sold. Our wines have been on the market since 2014.

Ivan Govorkowinemaker

Through the years

From grandpa’s vines to seven wines

  1. 50+ years ago

    Grandpa Branko plants the vines

    In Dusina he plants 8,000 vines of Plavka, Zlatarica, Maraština and Trbljan. Those vines still bear fruit today.

  2. For years

    The grapes go to middlemen

    The grapes from Dusina were sold at the markets. Wine was made only for the family.

  3. One morning

    Ten barrels outside the house

    A truck with ten used oak barrels pulls up outside the house in Metković. The barrels end up in the old butcher shop.

  4. 2014.

    Our first wines on the market

    Dišpet is our first wine. If it doesn’t sell, we’ll drink it. It sold.

  5. 2019.

    New vineyards

    Next to the old vines we plant Trnak, Pošip and Merlot. People told us Pošip would never ripen here.

  6. Today

    Seven wines

    From the sparkling Arija to Brankić from barrique, all from our own grapes.

Our goal is to return our native grapes to the place they deserve on the Croatian wine map.
