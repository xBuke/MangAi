Vrgorac wine region

Below the hills, among old vines.

The vineyards our grapes come from are more than fifty years old. Grandpa Branko planted the oldest vines, and we have no intention of pulling them out.

The Vrgorac wine region is one of the largest in Croatia. Our vines grow in Dusina, near Vrgorac, and we make the wine in Metković.

Alongside Plavka, Zlatarica Vrgorska, Maraština and Trbljan we have planted Trnak, Pošip and Merlot.
