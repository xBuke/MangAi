Pošip

pošip noun, grape variety

the one they told us would never ripen here.

We planted 2,000 vines in spite of everyone. It ripened.

Pošip comes from our native Dalmatian variety. Pale yellow. Citrus first on the nose, lemon and orange, then apricot. Seductive but never pushy. Dry and pleasantly fresh, with citrus notes, delicious and easy to drink.
