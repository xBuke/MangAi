Arija

arija noun, Dalmatian (It. aria)

air. A wine with a little air in it.

A sparkling wine from Plavka, a grape almost nobody makes it from.

Arija is made from our native Plavka. The grapes for the base wine were specially selected and spent 30 minutes on the skins. It was made by the traditional method, with a second fermentation in the bottle, then aged 24 months on the lees. Delicate pink, with fine, small bubbles, pleasant freshness, fruity and floral aromas and a scent of strawberry.
