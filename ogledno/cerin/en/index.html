Our story

A legacy born by the Adriatic Sea

What began in 1985 as a humble seaside eatery in Prižba has become a place guests return to year after year. Čerin blends 40 years of family tradition with modern Dalmatian cuisine.

Now the next generation carries the torch. We honour our ancestors’ time-tested recipes while bringing in modern techniques. Every plate is a tribute to the crystal-clear Adriatic, sun-kissed olive groves and the rich heritage of the Dalmatian coast.

Family legacy. Modern refinement.
