Our story

A legacy born by the Adriatic Sea

What began in 1985 as a humble seaside eatery in Prižba has become a place guests return to year after year. Čerin blends 40 years of family tradition with modern Dalmatian cuisine.

Now the next generation carries the torch. We honour our ancestors’ time-tested recipes while bringing in modern techniques. Every plate is a tribute to the crystal-clear Adriatic, sun-kissed olive groves and the rich heritage of the Dalmatian coast.

Family legacy. Modern refinement.

From sea to table

The journey of a catch

  1. 01 Sea

    The Adriatic off Prižba

    Prižba is a quiet bay on Korčula’s south coast, facing the open sea and the sunset.

  2. 02 Catch

    Fish of the day

    White fish is whatever the sea brings that day. It’s on the menu as catch of the day, priced per kilo.

  3. 03 Fire

    Wood-fired grill

    Fish and seafood are cooked on a wood-fired grill, simply, the way it has always been done on the island.

  4. 04 Table

    On your table

    With sailor-style potatoes, chard and a glass of Korčula Pošip, as the sun sets over Prižba.
