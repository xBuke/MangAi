About us

Sample text written for this demo. Not an official text of the agency.

What we do

The agency investigates accidents and serious incidents in air, maritime and rail transport. The purpose of every investigation is to establish what happened and why, and to recommend measures that can prevent similar events.

An investigation does not determine blame or liability. Proceedings of other bodies are conducted separately and independently of the safety investigation.

How we work

  1. Notification of an event. Events are reported by operators, infrastructure managers, crews, emergency services or members of the public.
  2. Decision to investigate. Based on the first information, the severity of the event and the safety lessons it may offer are assessed.
  3. Gathering facts. Site examination, recorder data, documentation, interviews.
  4. Interim statement. If the investigation takes longer, the facts confirmed so far are published, without conclusions on causes.
  5. Final report. Contains the facts, analysis, conclusions and safety recommendations.
  6. Follow-up of recommendations. Addressees report on the measures taken.

Organisation

The agency consists of the director's office, three investigation departments (air, maritime and rail) and shared services. Names and biographies are published in the real system; this demo contains no real persons.
